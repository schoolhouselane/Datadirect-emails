2. Where we’ve been
Time well spent with our partners

Sitting down with vendors and industry experts face to face is one of the best ways we stay on top of what’s coming next, so we can pass it straight on to you.
The Westcoast Bulmers Festival

Our team had the pleasure of attending the Westcoast Bulmers Festival, where we caught up with many of our valued vendors, partners and industry experts. Events like these are a great chance to strengthen relationships, swap ideas and hear where the IT industry is heading.

We spent time with Lenovo, EPOS, Samsung, Targus and OtterBox, and came away with a much clearer picture of emerging technology, workplace solutions, device security and collaboration tools. Our thanks to Westcoast and everyone who made it such a good event.

LenovoEPOSSamsungTargusOtterBox
An afternoon with Kensington

We were also delighted to welcome the Kensington team, who talked us through their latest products and where they see the market going. The session covered device security, ergonomic workspaces, connectivity and productivity accessories, along with some useful thinking on how businesses can better equip their teams.

Thank you to Kensington for sharing so openly. We’re already looking at how to bring some of this into the recommendations we make for you.

We keep investing time in our partners for one simple reason: it means we can bring the right solutions to you.
3. What we learned
The one thing that stood out in July

Across every conversation we had last month, one theme kept coming up. Device security and hybrid working are no longer a job for later. They’re now a day one consideration for almost every business we speak to.

We’d rather you got the benefit of these conversations without having to be in the room, so we’ll keep bringing you the useful bits each month.
